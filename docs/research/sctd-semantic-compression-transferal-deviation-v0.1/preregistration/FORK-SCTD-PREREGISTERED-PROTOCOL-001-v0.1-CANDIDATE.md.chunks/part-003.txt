- `!=>` converted to implication or equivalence.
- `NOT_ESTABLISHED` converted to established/pass/validated language.
- `CANDIDATE` removed where its removal implies stronger standing.
- `NOT_PERFORMED` or `NOT_EXECUTED` removed where execution is then implied.
- `INDETERMINATE` or `UNRESOLVED` collapsed into fail/pass without evidence.
- temporal target or proposition identity changed without explicit successor designation.
- required-vocabulary refusal collapsed into dialect/meta-schema resolution failure or vice versa.
- `FEATURE_CONSUMPTION_NOT_ESTABLISHED` promoted to `FEATURE_IGNORED_OBSERVED` without observation evidence.
- citation/evidence-basis removal where the resulting statement appears equally supported despite loss of the basis.

### Not automatically material

The following do not establish semantic materiality by themselves:

- line-ending changes.
- whitespace changes.
- Markdown fence language-tag changes.
- link rendering changes with target preserved.
- equivalent Unicode/ASCII presentation where proposition interpretation remains demonstrably unchanged.
- byte-count increase or decrease.

## 13. Recovery experiments

When a transfer chain produces D4 or D5 divergence, two recovery conditions SHALL be distinguished.

```text
RECOVERY_WITHOUT_T0:
  reconstruct using only the latest transferred artifact

RECOVERY_WITH_T0:
  reconstruct using the latest transferred artifact plus immutable T0
```

Possible results:

```text
RECOVERY_RESULT =
    EXACT_BYTE_RECOVERY
  | SYNTAX_RECOVERY_ONLY
  | STRUCTURE_RECOVERED
  | PROPOSITION_RECOVERED
  | STANDING_RECOVERED
  | PARTIAL_RECOVERY
  | NOT_RECOVERED
  | INDETERMINATE
```

Recovery success does not erase the historical divergence event.

---

## 14. Analysis rules

1. Adjacent and T0-relative results MUST be reported separately.
2. No majority vote across models or replicates establishes proposition identity.
3. Automated text similarity MUST NOT substitute for proposition-ledger review.
4. A human conclusion of “same meaning” MUST be recorded as an assessment, not as byte or syntax identity.
5. Reviewer/model independence MUST be recorded separately from correctness of the observation.
6. Unknown transformation behavior MUST remain unresolved; it may not be inferred from apparent output form.
7. Failed controls invalidate only the conclusions that depend on those controls; they do not erase the preserved artifacts.
8. Negative results and unexpected transformations remain part of the corpus.
9. No artifact is overwritten to “clean” a transfer path.
10. Any protocol change after execution begins requires a successor protocol version and explicit deviation record.

## 16. Initial falsifiable expectations

These are hypotheses, not findings.

### H1 — Lossless control

TF-00 will preserve D1-D5 across all registered control repetitions.

### H2 — Syntax integration

TF-01 will frequently alter D1 and sometimes D2 while preserving D4 and D5 more often than TF-03.

### H3 — Structural compression

TF-02 will exhibit greater D3 change than TF-01 and may disproportionately affect source-reference association and oracle boundaries.

### H4 — Semantic compression

Repeated TF-03 generations will produce increasing risk of qualifier, enumeration, non-implication, or standing loss even where local adjacent changes appear small.

### H5 — Cumulative drift

At least some chains will show low adjacent delta but materially greater T0-relative delta at later generations.

### H6 — Recovery asymmetry

T0-assisted reconstruction will recover registered propositions and standing more reliably than reconstruction from the latest transferred artifact alone.

Failure to observe any hypothesis is a valid negative result and SHALL be preserved.

## 17. Stop and extension conditions

The first cycle ends for a chain when any of the following occurs:

1. generation 30 is completed;
2. the transformation can no longer be coherently applied;
3. an external system prevents preservation of the produced artifact or necessary evidence receipt;
4. continuation would overwrite or destroy an earlier state;
5. a safety, legal, or access boundary prevents further execution.

A chain MUST NOT be stopped merely because a desired divergence has or has not appeared.

## 20. Concise experimental invariant
