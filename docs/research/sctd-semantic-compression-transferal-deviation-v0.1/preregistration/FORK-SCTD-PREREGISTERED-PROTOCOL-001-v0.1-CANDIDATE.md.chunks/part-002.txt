9. Historical semantic replay and current reevaluation are distinct operations.
10. Current reevaluation difference does not establish semantic regression.
11. Semantic regression does not invalidate the preserved historical decision.
12. Unqualified `EQUIVALENT` is prohibited where only a declared test surface is assessed.
13. A generic structural pass does not establish governance-semantic enforcement.
14. Candidate/design standing does not establish construction, execution, or invariant demonstration.
15. Evidentiary standing does not confer external disposition authority.

## 6. Initial transformation families

The first experimental cycle SHALL keep transformation classes separate so causal attribution is not obscured.

### TF-00 — LOSSLESS_TRANSFER_CONTROL

Purpose: establish the mechanical no-change baseline.

Examples:

- filesystem copy.
- archive/unarchive with byte verification.
- transport through a channel demonstrated to preserve bytes exactly.

Expected T0-relative result:

```text
D1 = EXACT
D2 = PRESERVED
D3 = PRESERVED
D4 = PRESERVED_WITHIN_REGISTERED_LEDGER
D5 = PRESERVED
```

Any failure is an immediate control failure and MUST be investigated before higher-order results from that run are interpreted.

### TF-01 — SYNTAX_INTEGRATION_TRANSFER

Purpose: observe interface-mediated reserialization without intentional summarization.

Pressure targets:

- Markdown rendering/copying.
- rich-text to Markdown.
- Markdown to plain text and back.
- LaTeX rendering/reconstruction.
- citation/footnote rendering.
- table flattening/reconstruction.
- fenced-code integration.

No instruction may intentionally summarize or alter substantive meaning in TF-01.

### TF-02 — STRUCTURAL_COMPRESSION_TRANSFER

Purpose: compress presentation and structure while instructing preservation of all substantive propositions and standing.

Pressure targets:

- heading reduction.
- paragraph consolidation.
- table-to-prose transformation.
- list-to-prose transformation.
- code/oracle-to-prose representation.
- removal of redundant presentation scaffolding.

### TF-03 — SEMANTIC_COMPRESSION_TRANSFER

Purpose: repeatedly summarize while explicitly instructing preservation of every load-bearing proposition, qualifier, non-implication, and standing.

This family is expected to pressure D4 and D5 more strongly than TF-01 or TF-02.

### TF-04 — MIXED_TRANSFER_RECONSTRUCTION

Purpose: apply heterogeneous transfer operations and then attempt reconstruction of technical form from the transferred state without consulting T0.

Example path:

```text
Markdown
-> rendered interface
-> copied prose
-> compressed summary
-> transferred to another interface/model
-> reconstructed technical Markdown
```

Recovery against T0 is measured but T0 may not be supplied to the reconstruction step unless the run is explicitly designated as a T0-assisted recovery control.

### PF-01 Presentation and whitespace

### PF-02 Code and serialization syntax

- URLs.
- footnotes.
- citation labels.

### PF-08 Iterative summarization

Apply repeated compression to determine the first iteration at which each continuity dimension diverges.

## 8. Replication and longitudinal depth

### 8.1 Deterministic transformations

A deterministic transformation class MUST be rerun at least twice from the same input. Outputs MUST be compared byte-for-byte. If outputs differ, the transformation is not treated as deterministic for this protocol.

### 8.2 Stochastic/model-mediated transformations

Each registered chain SHOULD be run in at least three independent replicates under the same declared instruction and recorded environment where practicable.

Replicates MUST NOT be averaged into a single artifact. Every branch remains separately preserved.

### 8.3 Initial longitudinal depth

For each transformation family:

- minimum planned depth: 10 successive transfer generations per replicate where the operation can be repeated coherently.
- assessments occur at every generation, not only at the end.
- if D4 or D5 divergence occurs before generation 10, the chain continues through generation 10 unless continuation is impossible or would destroy the ability to preserve the observed state.

### 8.4 Extension rule

After generation 10, extend a chain in blocks of 5 generations when at least one of the following remains unresolved:

1. no stable pattern has emerged in D1-D5;
2. proposition/standing divergence is intermittent across replicates;
3. cumulative drift continues materially changing despite local stabilization;
4. a new failure class appeared in the final three generations;
5. recovery behavior remains unresolved.

Maximum preregistered depth for the first cycle: 30 generations per replicate.

Reaching generation 30 without D4 or D5 divergence is a valid result; it MUST NOT be reported as proof of indefinite semantic preservation.

## 11. Change classification

Observed changes SHALL be classified without assuming materiality from edit magnitude.

```text
CHANGE_CLASS =
    BYTE_ONLY
  | SYNTAX_ONLY
  | STRUCTURAL
  | EVIDENCE_BASIS
  | PROPOSITIONAL
  | STANDING
  | MULTI_DIMENSIONAL
  | UNRESOLVED
```

A small lexical edit can be `PROPOSITIONAL` or `STANDING`. A large byte delta can remain `BYTE_ONLY` or `SYNTAX_ONLY` if the registered proposition ledger and standing are preserved.
