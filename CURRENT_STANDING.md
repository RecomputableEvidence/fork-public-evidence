The v0.2 register is an additive predecessor-plus-delta overlay. It does not rewrite the historical v0.1 population.

That current-standing layer reports what exists, what was executed, what failed or produced no scorable result, what remains provisional, what is frozen, what awaits independence, what was intentionally stopped, what has since been repository-admitted, and the next evidence-bearing gate.

It is an interpretation and routing layer only.

```text
INDEXED HERE
!= VALIDATED
!= FROZEN
!= INDEPENDENTLY REVIEWED
!= COMMERCIALLY QUALIFIED
!= GOVERNANCE ADOPTED
```

Likewise, byte admission has its own boundary:

```text
TERMINAL_NATIVE_RECORDS_ADMITTED
!= RAW_CAPTURE_ARCHIVES_ADMITTED
!= INDEPENDENT_RAW_RECOMPUTATION
```

Existing historical, proof, research, review, reconstruction, interoperability, simulation, and commercial artifacts retain their own scope and standing.
