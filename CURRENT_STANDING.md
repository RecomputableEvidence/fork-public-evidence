# Current Standing

For the repository-wide current program state, begin here:

- [`docs/current-standing/README.md`](docs/current-standing/README.md)

That current-standing layer reports what exists, what was executed, what failed or produced no scorable result, what remains provisional, what is frozen, what awaits independence, what was intentionally stopped, and the next evidence-bearing gate.

It is an interpretation and routing layer only.

```text
INDEXED HERE
!= VALIDATED
!= FROZEN
!= INDEPENDENTLY REVIEWED
!= COMMERCIALLY QUALIFIED
!= GOVERNANCE ADOPTED
```

Existing historical, proof, research, review, reconstruction, interoperability, simulation, and commercial artifacts retain their own scope and standing.
